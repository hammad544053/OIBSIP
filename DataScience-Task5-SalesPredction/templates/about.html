How it works

  1. Visitor inputs

    Nine features are collected from the prediction form.

  2. Feature scaling

    The fitted StandardScaler from training transforms submitted values.

  3. Classification

    The selected Logistic Regression model predicts class 0 or 1 and estimates click probability.

  4. Result & history

    The result is displayed and saved in a local CSV file.
